### Thai Peanut Chicken Wraps
source: [https://www.tampabay.com/things-to-do/food/cooking/12-easy-rollups-for-your-next-party_163720612/?outputType=amp](https://www.tampabay.com/things-to-do/food/cooking/12-easy-rollups-for-your-next-party_163720612/?outputType=amp)

#### Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup [[peanut butter]]
- 3 tablespoons + 1 tablespoon [[soy sauce]]
- 1 tablespoon [[rice wine vinegar]]
- 1 teaspoon [[Sriracha]]
- 1 teaspoon [[sesame oil]]
- 3 boneless, skinless [[chicken breasts]]
- 8 ounces softened [[cream cheese]]
- 1/2 teaspoon ground [[ginger]]
- 6 large [[tortillas]]
- [[romaine lettuce]]
- sliced [[red bell pepper]]

---
#### Directions:
- Make the peanut sauce by whisking together ½ cup peanut butter, 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon rice wine vinegar, 1 teaspoon Sriracha and 1 teaspoon sesame oil. Set aside.
- Slice ½-inch deep scores in 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts. In a large bowl toss the chicken with 1 tablespoon each soy sauce and vegetable oil. Heat a skillet over high heat and cook chicken for 4-6 minutes on each side, until cooked through. Slice the chicken into strips.
- Mix 8 ounces softened cream cheese and ½ teaspoon ground ginger into a small bowl together.
- Spread cream cheese mixture evenly over 6 large spinach flour tortillas, then add as much romaine lettuce and sliced red bell pepper you like. Top with chicken and peanut sauce.
