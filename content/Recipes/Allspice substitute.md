### Allspice substitute
source: [http://www.food.com/recipe/allspice-substitute-258672](http://www.food.com/recipe/allspice-substitute-258672)
#### Ingredients:
- [ ] 1/2 tsp [[cinnamon]]
- [ ] 1/2 tsp [[clove]] (or 1/4 tsp, see step 3 below)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp [[nutmeg]] (or 1/4 tsp, see step 3 below)

---
#### Directions:
1. Mix together; you want equal amounts of the spices above.
    
2. Use according to your recipe.
    
3. Note: If you like a lesser amount of cloves and nutmeg, the formula is 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cloves, 1/4 tsp nutmeg.
