#### Ingredients:
- 8 ounces softened [[cream cheese]]
- 1/2 cup [[pesto]]
- 1/3 cup shredded [[Parmesan]] cheese
- 4 [[tortillas]]
- deli [[turkey]] or [[ham]]

---
#### Directions:
In a bowl, beat together8 ounces softened cream cheese, ½ cup pesto, and ? cup shredded Parmesan cheese until smooth. Spread ? of the mixture evenly on top of 1 tortilla. Top that with 2 to 3 slices of deli turkey or ham. Repeat with two more tortillas. Roll up tortillas tightly, cover in plastic wrap and refrigerate. When ready to serve, unwrap and slice into rounds.
