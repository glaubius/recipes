### Bacon in the oven
source: [https://downshiftology.com/recipes/how-to-cook-bacon-in-the-oven/](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/how-to-cook-bacon-in-the-oven/)
tags: recipe
#### Ingredients:
- [ ] [[bacon]]

---
#### Directions:
Preheat your oven to 400 degrees F. Line a baking tray with parchment paper, then place the bacon side-by-side on the tray.

Cook the bacon for 18-20 minutes or until it's reached your desired level of crispiness. Remove the tray from the oven and transfer the bacon with tongs to a paper towel-lined plate.
